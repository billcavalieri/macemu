## Qualification status — October 11, 2026

The generated rows describe implemented paths and refusals. This section records what was checked against the ROM.

### Pattern fills (hook code 3), on by default

**What changed.** The first rule read the pattern from the port's fill pattern. That is right only for fill calls:
frames and paints use the pen pattern, erases the background pattern, and nothing in the block says which. In a Finder
and iTunes workload it accepted 0.2% of the pixels, and its mismatches (black fills, small draws) were this mistake
showing through. The block is QuickDraw's own drawing frame and holds the pattern the ROM is about to paint, already
expanded to the destination's pixels and resolved for the pen mode and colours (offsets in `nqd_fillmask.h`). The rule
now reads that, so solid colours and the desktop pattern are one case and the special exclusions are gone.

**Against the ROM** (pref `sheepforce_probe`: the ROM draws, and the plan the hook would have used is compared with
its pixels). Finder windows opened, overlapped, closed and raised, menus, Get Info, then iTunes opened and raised
against a Finder window, on a clone of the test disk at 1024x768, 32-bit:

| Result | Draws | Rectangle area |
|---|---|---|
| Exact | 447 | 35.4 Mpx |
| Drawn over before the comparison | 79 | 0.4 Mpx |
| Mismatch | 5 | 0.004 Mpx |
| Refused: XOR (pen 14, transfer 10) | 138 | 14.6 Mpx |
| Refused: other modes, not the framebuffer | 11 | 0.03 Mpx |

The comparison runs at the next hook call. An icon plotted on a square the fill has just erased goes through no hook,
so it is in the picture by then: "drawn over" are the draws where every differing pixel had been changed to something
else, and every pixel the later drawing left alone matched the plan. The 5 mismatches are the same icon squares with
one or two pixels where the new icon happens to equal the old one. No draw was found where the ROM painted the
pattern differently from the plan.

**On against off, same build.** The same scripted workload with the hook on and with `sheepforce_fillmask false`,
screenshots at 14 points: identical at every point except the Get Info window's size and date text, which differ
between any two runs. With the hook on: 231 draws, all on the GPU, none declined, none lost.

**A bug this exposed, fixed.** The GPU does a fill slightly after the hook returns. The ROM calls a code's sync
routine before it draws that code itself, but only for codes that have a hook, and text, icons and masked blits come
through codes that had none. They were drawn while a fill under them was in flight, and the fill then wiped them: a
newly opened window showed with a grey title, no scroll bars and its icon not selected. Codes 1, 4, 6, 7 and 8 now
have a hook that waits for the GPU and declines. This applied to the older rectangle fills and blits as well.

**Not done.** XOR fills (window drag outlines, selection rectangles) stay with the ROM; their rectangles are large but
the pixels they change are few. Codes 1, 4 and 6 (masked blits, text, icons) are not accelerated. The speed of the
change is measured below, not assumed.

**Speed.** Host CPU seconds of the VM process over 30 scripted raises of iTunes and 30 of a Finder window (about
108 s of wall time each run), alternating runs: on 53.3 and 56.2, off 54.8 and 53.4. No difference outside the
run-to-run spread. The hook itself spent 23 ms on 579 draws (8.0 Mpx) in such a run. Raising a window is bound by the
guest's own code, not by these fills, so the change is correct and free but is not a visible speedup here.

### Other paths

QD3D is qualified by `tools/run_sheepforce_tests.py` (143 checks against a fake guest) and the in-emulator self-test;
a real model was displayed once on the engine (October 4). QuickTime is measured separately.

| `ACCL_FILLRECT` | shader | solid fill (mode 8) and invert (mode 10) with no clip regions; otherwise the CPU path |
| `ACCL_FILLMASK` | shader | patterns as the ROM expanded them for the draw (block +0x294: pointer, row bytes, masks and position), so the pen, background and fill patterns and both pen modes are one case: a solid colour (sf_fillspans) or a tile of up to 256x256 pixels (sf_filltile; pref sheepforce_fillmask_tiles, on by default); transfer mode 8, pen mode 8 or 12, 32-bit framebuffer destination; clip is the intersection of the overlapping regions reached through handles at the block offsets 0x1a4/0x1a8/0x1ac/0x1e0/0x20c (undecodable ones skipped); CPU fallback. On by default (pref sheepforce_fillmask). XOR fills (pen 14, transfer 10) and arithmetic modes stay with the ROM. Evidence is in the qualification section |
| `ACCL_LINES` | shader | solid pen-colour rectangles (frames, rules) when a reachable clip region is found and none can clip them; otherwise the ROM draws. On by default (pref sheepforce_lines); the gate also follows direct region pointers, verified by the probe (5160 lines decoded, 0 mismatches, 0 clipped leaks in 810 gated lines) |
